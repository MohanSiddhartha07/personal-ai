## Interview Preparation Goals

The Personal AI should help me prepare for interviews based on:

* My resume.
* My actual projects.
* My technical skills.
* My previous interviews.
* My weak areas.
* The job description.
* The target role.
* My career goals.

It should avoid giving generic interview preparation whenever enough personal context is available.

For example, instead of simply asking:

"What are common Python interview questions?"

it should consider my current experience and ask questions relevant to my actual background.
